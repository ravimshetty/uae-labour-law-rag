Article (9) Probation Period
1. The employer may employ the worker under a probation period that does not exceed six months from the date of commencement of work.
2. The employer may terminate the employment of the worker during the probation period by giving written notice of at least 14 days.
3. A worker who wishes to move to another employer in the State during probation must notify the employer in writing at least one month in advance.

Article (17) Working Hours
1. The maximum normal working hours for workers in the private sector are eight hours per day or forty-eight hours per week.
2. Working hours are reduced during the holy month of Ramadan as determined by the Executive Regulation.

Article (19) Overtime
1. The employer may require the worker to work overtime provided that overtime does not exceed two hours per day.
2. A worker who works overtime is entitled to the basic wage for normal hours plus at least 25 percent of that wage.

Article (29) Annual Leave
1. The worker is entitled to paid annual leave of not less than 30 days for each year of service.
2. The worker is entitled to 2 days for each month if the service is more than six months and less than one year.

Article (30) Maternity Leave
1. A female worker is entitled to maternity leave of 60 days, of which 45 days are with full wage and 15 days with half wage.

Article (31) Sick Leave
1. After completing the probation period the worker is entitled to sick leave of not more than 90 days per year.
2. The first 15 days are paid with full wage, the next 30 days with half wage, and the remaining days are unpaid.

Article (43) Termination of the Employment Contract by Notice
1. Either party may terminate the employment contract for a legitimate reason by giving written notice to the other party.
2. The notice period agreed in the contract shall be not less than 30 days and not more than 90 days.

Article (51) End of Service Gratuity
1. A full-time foreign worker who completes one year or more of continuous service is entitled to an end of service gratuity at the end of service.
2. The gratuity is calculated on the basic wage as follows: 21 days of wage for each year of the first five years of service, and 30 days of wage for each year beyond five years.
3. The total gratuity shall not exceed the wage of two years.
