PART 1: GENERAL

14. Right to a written contract
(1) An Employer shall provide an Employee with a written employment contract.
(2) The contract shall state, among other things, the length and conditions of any probationary period, which shall not exceed six months.

27. Vacation Leave
(1) An Employee who has been employed for at least 90 days is entitled to at least 20 Work Days of paid Vacation Leave in each Vacation Leave Year.
(2) An Employee may carry over at least 5 Work Days of unused Vacation Leave to the following Vacation Leave Year.

34. Sick Leave
(1) An Employee who has completed the probation period is entitled to up to 60 Work Days of sick leave in any 12 month period.

62. Minimum notice periods
(1) Either party may terminate the employment contract by giving written notice.
(2) The minimum notice period is 7 days where continuous employment is less than 3 months, 30 days where it is 3 months or more but less than 5 years, and 90 days where it is 5 years or more.

66. Gratuity Payment and Qualifying Scheme Benefits
(1) Since the introduction of the Qualifying Scheme, the Employer makes monthly contributions for each eligible Employee into a Qualifying Scheme such as DEWS instead of accruing an end of service gratuity.
(2) The monthly contribution is 5.83 percent of the Employee's basic wage during the first five years of service and 8.33 percent thereafter.
